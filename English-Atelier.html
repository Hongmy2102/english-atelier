# IPA pronunciation data

The General American IPA subset in phonetics.js is derived from:
https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict (data/en_US.txt).

The upstream README attributes the US data to lingz/cmudict-ipa with stress markers from kylebgorman/syllabify. British spellings are mapped to their US equivalents where appropriate. A small number of missing forms and the Education mindmap citation forms have editorial transcriptions. No UK/RP dataset is included.

IPA entries are dictionary citation forms. Joining entries for a phrase does not transcribe connected speech or guarantee that a selected synthetic voice uses exactly that pronunciation. Pronunciation audio is produced by the user's browser/OS speech engine, not recordings from Cambridge or another dictionary.
